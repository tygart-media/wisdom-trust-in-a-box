# The Wisdom Trust — facility pilot one-pager

**For:** facility directors, activities directors, regional operators
**Read time:** 3 minutes

## The idea in one sentence

Offer every resident a private life-story collection — one emailed question a week, answered by tapping a link and talking — and give your facility the one amenity nobody else has.

## How it works

- A family member emails one question a week (from a library of 40, or their own).
- The resident taps **one link**. No app, no account. They talk or type.
- Stories save to the family's private, encrypted vault. The resident owns everything and can delete anything.
- Staff time: one champion (activities director is ideal), roughly an hour a week for ten residents.

## The 30-day pilot

- **10 residents**, volunteer basis, consent signed.
- **4 questions**, one per week.
- **Day 30:** play highlights back for the families and ask one question — *was this worth it?*
- Cost: near zero. Tooling is commodity; the kit is open source.

## Why you'd do this

Touring families choose on feeling, not floor plans. "Every resident here gets their life captured — your mother's stories, in her voice, for your grandchildren" is a feeling no competitor's dining menu can match. It deepens family loyalty, drives word-of-mouth, and costs less than the lobby flowers.

## What we need from you

One staff champion, ten willing residents, and permission to ask their families one question a week. We bring the kit, the questions, and the consent paperwork.
