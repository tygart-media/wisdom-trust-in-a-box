# Why facilities want this — the economics, one page

## The move-in decision is emotional

Families choosing a facility are buying peace of mind and voting with their hearts. Square footage and dining menus are table stakes — every decent facility has them. Differentiation wins tours, and tours fill beds.

A life-story program is differentiation that photographs well, talks well, and *feels* different from everything else on the tour: "Every resident here gets their life captured. Your mother's stories, in her voice, preserved for your grandchildren. It's part of living here."

## The math

- **Cost per resident:** near zero. One link a week, commodity tooling, ~6 minutes of staff attention per resident per week.
- **A 10-resident pilot:** ~1 staff hour/week for 4 weeks. Total incremental cost: trivial.
- **A filled bed:** worth tens of thousands a year. **One** family choosing your facility over the competitor because of this program pays for the program a thousand times over.
- **Retention:** families emotionally invested in an ongoing story collection are stickier. Churn drops when leaving means abandoning grandma's archive.

## The word-of-mouth engine

Nobody posts "the dining hall was adequate" — but families *will* share "listen to what my dad recorded this week." Every story a family replays is marketing you can't buy, carrying your facility's name into exactly the demographic that chooses facilities next.

## The risk side (honest version)

- **Privacy must be airtight.** The consent template makes the resident the owner, full stop. One mishandled recording kills the program. Treat recordings like medical records.
- **Don't overpromise the tech.** No "talk to grandma forever" demos until a product earns it independently. The pitch is the *collection*, not the twin.
- **Staff can't be the bottleneck.** The one-link flow exists so staff guide, not operate. If it needs IT support per resident, redesign.

## The line for the board

*For less than the cost of the lobby flowers, we can offer families the one thing no competitor has: their parent's life, preserved. Ten residents, thirty days, then we decide with evidence.*
