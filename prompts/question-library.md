# Question Library — 40 prompts across a life

Use: send one per week by email (see `onboarding/one-link-flow.md`). Let the storyteller skip anything. The best stories come from the prompts they *want* to answer, not the ones you think matter.

## Childhood

1. What did your street sound like when you were ten?
2. Who was the adult (not a parent) who shaped you most as a kid?
3. What did your family do when money was tight?
4. What was the first thing you ever bought with your own money?
5. Describe a regular Sunday in your childhood home.
6. What got you in the most trouble as a kid — and was it worth it?
7. What did you want to be when you grew up, before anyone told you to be realistic?

## Work

8. What was your first real job, and what did it teach you that school didn't?
9. Tell me about the best boss you ever had. What did they do differently?
10. Tell me about the worst one. What did you learn from surviving them?
11. What's a skill from your working life that young people today don't seem to learn?
12. Describe a day at work you'll never forget — good or bad.
13. What would you do differently about your career if you could replay it?
14. What did your work cost you that was worth it? What cost you that wasn't?

## Love & family

15. How did you meet your spouse / partner? Tell it like a story.
16. What did your parents get right that you've tried to copy?
17. What did they get wrong that you've tried not to repeat?
18. What's something about raising kids that nobody warned you about?
19. Describe a moment you knew your family would be okay.
20. Who in the family is the keeper of the stories now — and are they ready?

## Hard times

21. Tell me about the hardest year of your life. What got you through it?
22. What's a failure that turned out to be a gift?
23. Was there a moment you had to start over? What did you take with you?
24. What do you wish someone had told you when you were struggling?
25. How did you learn to forgive someone — or did you?

## Joy

26. What was the happiest ordinary day you can remember?
27. What meal, made by whom, do you still think about?
28. What song takes you straight back somewhere? Where?
29. Describe a place in the world that felt like it was yours.
30. What made you laugh the hardest you ever laughed?

## Wisdom

31. What do you know now that you wish you'd known at 30?
32. What's a belief you held strongly and then changed your mind about?
33. What does a good life actually require? (Fewer things than people think, usually.)
34. What should your grandchildren spend less time worrying about?
35. What should they spend *more* time on?
36. If you could leave your family one sentence — not advice, just truth — what would it be?

## The facility chapter

37. What surprised you most about this stage of life?
38. Who here has become an unexpected friend?
39. What do you want the staff here to know about who you were before you arrived?
40. Finish this: "If someone reads this in fifty years, I want them to know ___."
