## Your Stories, Your Rules

**What this is:** [Facility name] is offering residents the chance to record life stories — by talking or typing, one question at a time. Your recordings are saved privately for you and the family members you choose.

**You own your stories.** Every recording belongs to you. You can listen to, change, or delete any of them at any time, for any reason, no questions asked.

**You choose who hears them.** Nobody — not staff, not other residents, not family — can access your recordings unless you say so. You can grant and revoke access whenever you like.

**You can skip or stop.** Skip any question. Stop the program entirely. Deleting your recordings is permanent and immediate.

**What we won't do:** We will never sell your recordings, use your voice to train products without your written permission, or share your stories publicly (including "success story" marketing) without your explicit, separate, written okay.

**What happens if you pass away:** Your designated person below decides what happens to your collection — keep it in the family, share selections, or delete it. If no one is designated, the collection is deleted after [12 months].
